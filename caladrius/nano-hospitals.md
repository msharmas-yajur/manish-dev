## Executive Summary

Nano Hospitals is a NABH-accredited multi-specialty healthcare institution operating in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Established approximately 14 years ago (~2010-2012), the hospital chain positions itself as "The Patient's Choice" with a focus on world-class infrastructure and patient-first care delivery. The organization operates two facilities in South Bangalore with a combined bed capacity of approximately 50-54 beds.
